Privacy Policy

Epoch Digitech · last updated 1 November 2026

What this tool is

Epoch Digitech operates an internal tool that publishes educational videos we produce ourselves to our own TikTok account, @epoch_ng. It is not a product offered to the public. It has no users other than us, no sign-up, and no user interface that anyone outside our organisation can reach.

What we collect

From TikTok, only what is required to upload a video to our own account:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token for our own TikTok account
  • The account identifier (open_id) TikTok returns with them
  • The basic profile information covered by the user.info.basic scope

We collect nothing from any other TikTok user. We do not read comments, messages, followers, or anyone else's content through this tool.

How it is stored

Credentials are held as encrypted secrets in the private GitHub repository that runs the tool, and are available only to that repository's automated jobs. They are not written to disk in the repository, not logged, and not copied anywhere else.

What we do with it

One thing: upload videos we created to our own TikTok drafts. We do not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to third parties. There is no advertising, profiling or analytics in this tool.

Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept while the tool is in use and deleted when it is retired. Access can be withdrawn at any time from TikTok's own settings, under Security and permissions, which immediately invalidates the tokens above.

Third parties

The tool runs on GitHub Actions and calls TikTok's Content Posting API. Those two services process the data described above in the course of running the tool. No one else receives it.

Children

This is an internal business tool and is not directed at children.
